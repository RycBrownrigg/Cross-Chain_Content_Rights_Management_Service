\begin{abstract}

The content creator economy concentrates value in centralized intermediaries. No existing system, centralized or decentralized, provides subscription access, pay-per-view consumption, and permanent ownership through a single rights primitive that is portable across heterogeneous blockchain networks.

We ask how a shared-security, multi-chain framework built on Polkadot XCM and FRAME primitives can deliver a unified rights record supporting all three monetization models, and what transaction finality, cost efficiency, and creator revenue retention it can achieve. We built the Cross-Chain Content Rights Management Service (CCRMS), a Polkadot parachain prototype, and evaluated it through unit testing, performance benchmarks, a security audit, a centralized baseline comparison, and a Monte Carlo creator-revenue simulation.

On a local test network, the prototype sustains at least 26.2 transactions per second, the rate our benchmark client could offer, with approximately six-second inclusion latency, and cross-chain rights operations reach the destination chain in a median of five blocks (about 30~s). In simulation, mean creator revenue is 4.4\% higher than on centralized platforms, but the median simulated creator earns less: CCRMS usually wins only for creators above roughly 6,000 followers, and the advantage disappears if its fee exceeds about 7\% or centralized discovery is about 25\% stronger than assumed. The prototype bounds the number of rights holders per content item, and this bound must be lifted before the advantage can be realized for larger audiences.

We conclude that a unified rights record supporting all three access models can be built from standard FRAME primitives, and that the standard XCM \texttt{WithdrawAsset}~$\to$ \texttt{BuyExecution}~$\to$ \texttt{Transact} sequence, available since XCM~v3, suffices for issuance-style cross-chain rights operations without custom protocol extensions. The revenue advantage of decentralized rights management is small and not universal; it depends on audience size and on the assumed value of centralized discovery.

\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} cross-chain content rights, Polkadot XCM, unified rights record, creator revenue retention, design science research
