\chapter{Monte Carlo Simulation Parameters and Sensitivity}\label{app:monte-carlo}

This appendix documents the full parameter set and sensitivity results for the Monte Carlo revenue simulation described in Section~\ref{sec:economic-results}. The simulation is seeded (xoshiro128**, seed~42) and exactly reproducible from the published repository.

The headline run used 1,000 creators $\times$ 10,000 iterations. The sensitivity and sweep results below used 200 creators $\times$ 2,000 iterations with the same seed; the baseline advantage reads 17.0\% against the 16.8\% in the headline results due to the smaller grid.

Table~\ref{tab:mc-params-platform-app} documents the per-platform parameters drawn uniformly once per iteration.

\begin{table}[h]

\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.20\linewidth} p{0.13\linewidth} p{0.18\linewidth} p{0.17\linewidth} p{0.13\linewidth}@{}}

\textbf{Platform} & \textbf{Fee} & \textbf{Discovery added} & \textbf{Monthly churn} & \textbf{Cost/tx} \\
\midrule
Centralized      & 30--45\% & +500--3,000 & 2--5\%   & \$0.000 \\
Web3 marketplace & 7.5--15\% & +100--800  & 5--10\%  & \$0.005 \\
Bridge-based     & 8--15\%  & +50--400   & 6--12\%  & \$0.020 \\
CCRMS            & 1--5\%   & +0--200    & 4--8\%   & \$0.001 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Discovery is additive rather than multiplicative: a flat bonus of 500--3,000 followers matters substantially at 200 followers and marginally at 50,000, which is what produces the audience-size crossover.

The CCRMS fee of 1--5\% is a conservative allowance for protocol or treasury fees rather than a measured cost; Polkadot fees are not levied as a share of revenue, and the model charges CCRMS per-transaction costs separately.

\section*{G.6 Reproduction Notes}

To reproduce the fee sweep: set \texttt{PLATFORMS.ccrms.feeLow} and \texttt{feeHigh} to the same fixed value in \texttt{scripts/perf/monte-carlo-revenue.mjs} and re-run.

To reproduce the discovery sweep: multiply \texttt{PLATFORMS.centralized.discoveryAddLow} and \texttt{discoveryAddHigh} by the desired factor.

Both sweeps use \texttt{nCreators: 200, nIterations: 2\_000} and \texttt{createRng(42)}, matching the existing \texttt{sensitivityAnalysis()} grid.
