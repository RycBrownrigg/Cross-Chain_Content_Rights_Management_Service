\chapter{Results}\label{chap:results}

This chapter reports the measured results. Section~\ref{sec:technical-results} covers technical results, Section~\ref{sec:economic-results} covers economic results, and Section~\ref{sec:security-user-results} covers security and user-centric results. We defer interpretation to Chapter~\ref{chap:discussion}.

\section{Technical Results}\label{sec:technical-results}

\subsection{Throughput}\label{subsec:throughput}

We evaluated all 11 local extrinsic types across four batch sizes (1, 10, 50, 100). At a batch size of 100, extrinsics without nesting constraints achieved a throughput of 16.6 TPS, whereas those involving the minting of child NFTs (\texttt{subscribe}, \texttt{purchase\_ownership}, \texttt{enable\_auto\_renew}, \texttt{disable\_auto\_renew}, \texttt{transfer\_ownership}) were limited to 8.3 TPS due to the maximum children constraint (\texttt{MaxChildren=50}), rather than by block weight. The extrinsics in Phase~4 (\texttt{set\_royalty\_splits}, \texttt{enable\_auto\_renew}, \texttt{disable\_auto\_renew}, \texttt{query\_rights\_metadata}) achieved throughput comparable to that of the baseline extrinsics, confirming that the unified rights token model incurs no measurable performance overhead.

TPS scales linearly with batch size, given a 6-second block time and one block per batch. During the sustained stress test, 200 concurrent accounts submitted \texttt{register\_content} transactions over 180 seconds, achieving a sustained throughput of \textbf{26.2 TPS} (corresponding to 174.6 transactions per block across 27 blocks). The utilization of block weight at 300 transactions was only \textbf{12.9\% of \texttt{ref\_time}} and \textbf{2.2\% of \texttt{proof\_size}}, indicating an estimated theoretical maximum of approximately \textbf{283 TPS} per parachain. The stress test experienced a transaction failure rate of 78\%, primarily due to RPC-layer nonce conflicts rather than parachain failures; all successfully included transactions were processed accurately. In a production environment, nonce contention would be mitigated by implementing a transaction sequencer or explicit nonce management.

This constitutes the most economically significant finding of the entire dissertation. \subsection{Test Coverage}\label{subsec:test-coverage}

Our unit test suite encompasses \textbf{16 of the 19 distinct error variants} defined in \texttt{pallet-content-rights}, achieving a coverage ratio of 84 percent, compared to the Phase~2 objective of 80 percent. \subsection{Static Analysis and Dependency Audit}\label{subsec:static-analysis}

Static analysis conducted using \texttt{cargo clippy} reported zero warnings within the dissertation's codebase. The \texttt{cargo audit} reported 8 advisories, all pertaining to transitive dependencies of the Polkadot SDK, including components such as networking, the WebAssembly (WASM) executor, Transport Layer Security (TLS), and logging. Importantly, none of these advisories affect our pallet code. These issues are effectively mitigated by the architectural separation between the sandboxed WASM runtime and the node binary. See Appendix~\ref{app:security} for the full advisory breakdown.
