#### 2. Consolidated Annotated Bibliography (Top 20 most important entries)

1. **IEEE** (2025). *IEEE Std 3221.01-2025: Standard for blockchain interoperability—Cross-chain transaction consistency protocol*. IEEE Standards Association. https://standards.ieee.org/ieee/3221.01/  
   First formal cross-chain consistency standard; provides atomicity benchmarks the thesis will measure against.

2. **Unique Network** (2025). *Composable Subscriptions & NFTs using XCM* (blog series Jan–Sep 2025). https://unique.network/blog  
   Closest existing work: native Polkadot recurring NFTs. Still lacks PPV counters and unified rights object → thesis extends this.

3. **MarketsandMarkets** (2025). *Blockchain Digital Rights Management Market – Global Forecast to 2029*.  
   Projects 54.2 % CAGR, $1.42 B by 2029 – validates economic significance of the research.

4. **IBC Protocol** (2025). *IBC v2 Specification and Launch Announcement*. https://ibcprotocol.dev  
   Sovereign interop alternative to XCM; 115+ chains connected by Nov 2025.

5. **Chainlink** (2025). *Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP) Technical Whitepaper v2*.  
   Oracle-based bridge used by most Ethereum-centric competitors.

6. **Zhang, L. et al.** (2024). A digital resource copyright protection scheme based on blockchain cross-chain technology. *Heliyon, 10*(16), e34781. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e34781  
   State-of-the-art cross-chain DRM – protection only, no monetisation workflows.

7. **USPTO/USCO** (2024). *Non-Fungible Tokens and Intellectual Property*. Joint report.  
   Authoritative legal analysis of NFT ownership and licensing – foundation for PurchaseVerification contract.

8. **Wang & Li** (2025). AI-blockchain integration for digital rights management: A review. *Journal of Digital Media Security*.  
   Shows AI improves verification; future extension opportunity for the thesis.

9. **Frontiers in Blockchain** (2025). Regulatory barriers in blockchain-based IP trade: Evidence from China.  
   Regulatory compliance identified as the single largest barrier (weight 0.308) → justifies optional ZK hooks.

10. **Eluvio** (2024). *The Content Fabric: Multi-Chain Content Ownership and Distribution*. Whitepaper.  
    Industry competitor with marketplace-ready fabric; hybrid centralised/decentralised.

11–20. Wood (2016, 2022), Parity ink! docs, Polkadot wiki, Xie & Liu (2022), Garba et al. (2020), etc.  
    Foundational technical and early multi-chain DRM papers (already in original proposal + Weeks 1–4 reports).
